Why phases happen every month

  • The Sun always illuminates half of the Moon.
  • As the Moon orbits Earth, we see different fractions of that illuminated half.
  • New, quarter, gibbous, and full phases occur every lunar month.

Why eclipses do not happen every month

  • The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5.1° relative to the Earth–Sun plane.
  • Most New and Full Moons occur above or below the exact eclipse alignment.
  • An eclipse requires New or Full Moon and the Moon being near an orbital node.
