The Earth-view Moon is rendered from the actual illumination geometry rather than a preset phase picture. Half of the Moon is always illuminated by the Sun; the slider changes how much of that illuminated half faces Earth.

What causes ordinary Moon phases?

  • The Sun always illuminates half of the Moon.
  • The Moon orbits Earth.
  • Our viewing angle changes, so we see different fractions of the illuminated half.
  • Earth's shadow is not responsible for ordinary phases.

What causes a lunar eclipse?

  • A lunar eclipse requires a full Moon.
  • The Sun, Earth, and Moon must also line up near an orbital node.
  • The Moon then passes through Earth's shadow.
  • The 5° orbital tilt is why this does not happen every month.
